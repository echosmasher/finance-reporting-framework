Executive Summary

Revenue tracked slightly ahead of Budget in January (+2.1%), a clean start to the year. The month's headline swing — EBITDA down 97% and Pre-tax Income negative — is not an operating problem: it's the annual Property & Liability Insurance premium and external audit fee, both invoiced in full this month per the usual January pattern, landing against a Budget that spreads them evenly across the year. GOP, the metric that actually reflects operating performance, came in only modestly under Budget (-2.7%).
