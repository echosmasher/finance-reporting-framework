Flagged Items

▲ INVESTIGATE Rooms – Payroll & Related

Rooms Payroll landed at 797,940 DKK against a Budget of 638,352 DKK — 25% over, while occupancy was 64.2% vs. a budgeted 65.0%, essentially flat. Payroll should move with occupancy, not diverge from it this sharply. The drill-down shows why: two overtime postings for Front Office and Housekeeping ("covering extended staff leave") plus a temporary housekeeping labor charge from Nordic Staffing Partners, together accounting for roughly 180,700 DKK of the overage. Check whether the underlying staff leave was planned or unplanned, and whether the current mix of overtime plus an external staffing agency is the most cost-effective way to cover it going forward — the agency line alone is a meaningful chunk of the gap.
