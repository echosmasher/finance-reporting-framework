INVESTIGATE Utilities

Utilities landed at 391,068 NOK against a Budget of 491,144 NOK — 20.4% under. Confirm the February electricity invoice was actually received and posted: the drill-down shows only a 5,267 NOK "estimated accrual" for Electricity this month, while every other utilities account (heating, water, waste, fuel) is running at its normal level. A gap this size, concentrated in a single account, usually means the invoice hasn't arrived yet rather than genuine savings — expect this to reverse, and likely overshoot, once it's billed.
